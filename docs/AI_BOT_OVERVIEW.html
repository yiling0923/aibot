AI Bot for LiveChat

The bot answers customers' common questions, instantly and in their own language, using only the answers the merchant wrote. When it is not sure, a person takes over.

1The idea in four rules

  • It only uses the merchant's own answers. Nothing from the internet, nothing made up.
  • It never guesses. No written answer means a person answers.
  • It asks once. If it does not understand, it asks the customer to say more, one time. Then a person takes over.
  • Each merchant is separate. Its own answers, its own settings, its own chats.

Why: agents answer the same questions all day (deposit time, bonus, password). The bot takes those, so agents keep the hard ones.

Clear question

The bot answers at once.

Unclear question

The bot asks once, with up to 3 choices. The customer taps one or types "2".

Still unclear, or wants a person

The chat goes to an agent. After that, the bot stays silent.

Rude customer

The bot replies kindly first. It closes the chat only if it continues.

4When a person takes over

  • The customer asks for an agent.
  • A complaint or dispute ("money not received", "scam").
  • An account or phone number is typed in the chat.
  • The bot did not understand, after the number of tries the merchant chose (0 to 3, normally 1).
  • The answer is marked needs staff (a person must answer it).
  • The bot is too slow (more than 8 seconds).

5Each merchant is separate

  • Its own database: answers, settings and chat history are never mixed with another merchant.
  • Its own bot key. The key tells the bot which merchant is asking.
  • The bot is off for a merchant until its key is put into that merchant's LiveChat settings. Adding one merchant never changes the others.

6How the answers are written

Topic and example questions

Each answer has a topic and at least 3 example questions, so the bot can find it.

Six languages

English, Malay, Chinese, Vietnamese, Thai, Filipino.

Three types

Answer. Needs staff. Promotion, with an end date so it hides itself.

Guided answers

After an answer, ask for an amount, username or phone, then pass the chat to a person with the details as a note.

Draft, then publish. Nothing is live until the merchant presses Publish. Each merchant keeps its own versions and can go back to an earlier one.

8Starting a new merchant

  1. Create the merchant in LiveChat.
    LiveChat and the bot keep separate lists of merchants.
  2. Add it in the bot panel.
    Name, main language, tone, and a staff login. It starts with no answers.
  3. Make the bot key and put it, with the bot address, into that merchant's AI Bot page in LiveChat.
  4. Check the settings. The defaults already work.
  5. Write the answers. Load the starter pack, then replace every TO WRITE: text with the real answer. The bot ignores an entry until it is written.
  6. Test as a customer, as a visitor and as a member.
  7. Go live. Switch the AI bot on for that merchant in LiveChat.
  8. Improve every week from the Report: write the missing answers, then publish.

A Setup checklist on the merchant's page turns each line green as it is done.

9Safety and control

  • No written answer means a hand-over, never a guess.
  • Staff can edit their own merchant only. Some settings are for admins only.
  • Nothing goes live without Publish, and a merchant can always go back.
  • The AI is optional. The bot works with its rules alone. When on, the AI only picks among approved answers or helps staff translate. It never writes facts.
  • Phone numbers and names typed into a guided answer are not kept in the bot's logs. The merchant sets how long chats are kept.

10Measuring and improving

  • The Report shows what share of messages the bot answered, did not understand, or handed to a person.
  • Staff mark answers right or wrong. This gives the wrong-answer rate.
  • Words customers type that the bot does not know yet are collected for the merchant to sort.
  • The weekly loop: read the Report, write the missing answers, publish.

Starting targets from the plan, to adjust after the pilot: most questions answered by the bot, about 85% accuracy, wrong answers at most 3%, asking back at most 15%.

11What works today, honestly

Works with LiveChat as it is

  • Answers, choices and hand-over
  • Guided answers (amount, username, phone, short reply)
  • The whole panel, many merchants, six languages

Needs other people

  • A public https address for the bot
  • The LiveChat team to confirm how LiveChat keeps replies by the question words (it can show one customer another's reply for short words like "2")
  • A first test with a real AI key

Not in this phase

  • Account answers (balance, deposits). LiveChat does not tell the bot who the customer is, and the account data in the test setup is demo data. These questions go to a person. They need the merchant's real API and a way to check the customer is who they say.
  • Pictures. LiveChat sends them straight to the agents.
  • Alerts when the bot is down. If the bot server is down, customers get no bot reply, and agents still see the chat.

12Pilot plan and decisions needed

  1. Pick one merchant for the pilot.
  2. Give it a bot key, load the starter pack, write the real answers, test, and go live.
  3. Watch the first chats. Review the Report every week.
  4. Add the next merchants the same way, using the setup checklist.

We need a decision on

  • Which merchant goes first.
  • Who writes and approves the answers.
  • A public address for the bot.
  • Asking the LiveChat team about the answer memory.
  • Whether to ask for each merchant's real API, for account answers in a later phase.

Questions you may be asked

Will it make mistakes? It can pick the wrong answer. Wrong answers are marked in the Report and fixed. When it is unsure, it hands over.

Does it replace agents? No. It takes the repeated questions. Anything unclear goes to a person.

Is the data safe? Each merchant's data is in its own database. Account data, when connected later, is read live and never saved.

What does it cost? The rules cost nothing. Only the optional AI costs money. The panel shows the cost per merchant, with a limit you can set.

What if a merchant wants a different answer? They edit it in the panel and press Publish. It is live within seconds.
